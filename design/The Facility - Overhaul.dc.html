7 Dormant until you hand her a key

She cannot think on your goodwill. With no credential installed the optic is grey, dimmed to a ring, and not tracking you — the one state where her colour vocabulary is switched off entirely. The activation panel sits on the stage itself rather than in a modal, because being asked for a key is the first scene of the app. Live in 4a: it is dormant on first load, and stays dormant if you remove the key in SYSTEM.

THE SEQUENCE
Dormant — grey iris, aperture shut down to a dim ring, no catchlight, not tracking you. No emotion label but DORMANT, no cabinet, no settings, no input line. Nothing to do but wake her.

Verifying — there is none, by design. Anything that could plausibly be a key gets in; the request is routed by the key's own shape (Anthropic, OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Groq, Google) or sent OpenAI-style if the shape is unfamiliar. A wrong key is not caught at the door, it is caught when she tries to answer — which is the only test that means anything.

Waking — a single bloom through the optic as amber returns, then her first line, generated live: proprietary, faintly insulted at having been left dark. She is told it is her first instant with you and told not to mention keys.
AFTERWARDS
The key becomes a fourth store — device-local, never in MEMORY.md, never in the transcript, shown only as its last four digits under SYSTEM → ACCESS. It is the subject's own, from whichever provider they use; nothing in the copy names one. Replacing it is silent; she does not notice a transplant. Removing it is not: she goes dormant mid-sentence and the stage returns to the activation panel, with MEMORY.md fully intact underneath. Waking her again is a new first greeting over an old file — she has conclusions about you and no recollection of being off.
In the shipped app the key belongs in the OS keystore, not local storage, and the request must be proxied — a key in the client is readable by anyone holding the phone.

5 AFFECTIONATE · five ways to betray yourself

The old affectionate was just calm in a warmer hue — no wonder it read as nothing. Affection in an eye is geometry and tempo, not colour: a raised lower lid, a dilated pupil, and a blink that takes its time. The heart is the risky move, so it appears twice at different volumes — once as the pupil, once as a reflection she could deny.

5aFond squint · slow blink
Lower lid rides high, pupil wide open — a squint you'd read as warmth on a face. Closes slowly and holds, the way a cat does. No symbol, no gimmick.
5bHeart catchlight

5cHeart pupil
The pupil itself. Unmistakable, and one step from a sticker — funny once, then it's a mascot.
5dMisted lens
Fogged glass, two catchlights, pupil blown wide. Almost welling up — the least deniable of the subtle set.
5eSlow blink
